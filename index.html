Our Mission

Hello, we are the FTC Robotics team of Garibaldi Secondary School, and our mission is to empower every student to explore and understand the real-world applications of technology in an inclusive and supportive environment.

About FTC

The FIRST Tech Challenge, or FTC, is a robotics competiton held by FIRST. Teams of students in grades 7-12 build and program a robot to compete in a new challenge announced every year in September.

The FIRST Tech Challenge allows students to learn about STEM concepts and develop an interest in robotics and technology, while learning teamwork, creativity, and problem solving.

Our Values

Teamwork

  • Practice leadership and organization in a primarily student-led environment
  • Learn to communicate effectively and develop ideas

Education

  • Learn about physics, engineering, computer science, and more
  • Understand real-world problem solving and applications of technology

Creativity

  • Practice problem-solving, critical thinking, and resource management
  • Design promotional materials to recruit members or get sponsors

Join Us!

Are you interested in mechanical engineering, designing, or just want to have fun? Join us to learn about robotics, participate in competitions, and have fun! All grades are welcome, no previous experience required.
