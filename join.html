Join Bear Necessities!

What we're looking for

We look for people who can create and innovate. It doesn't mean that your interests must be towards robotics or engineering instead we want to try and incorporate your interests and hobbies.

Artists

You could design and draw diagrams for our robot, or create presentations and posters. You could even make crafts to share with other teams during tournaments to foster good relationships.

Photographers

Document our progress and work on camera throughout the season. Feature your photos on the website and in our presentation for the judges or posters around the school.

Creative Writing

Use your writing skills to create a portfolio and script for the judge's presentation, documenting important details, our design and building process, and challenges.

Social Media & Communication

Work with photographers and document the process and send updates to sponsors or post on our social media platforms to showcase our progress and connect with other teams. You could reach out to potential sponsors and pitch sponsorship proposals.

Requirements

Even if you only do things related to your hobbies or interest, we still expect you to show up to meetings on time, stay for the full meeting and attend competitions. Lastly, you should still try to be involved with building the robot as much as possible, and always be ready to help.

Join Us!

When?

Every Monday, 2:45 PM to 5:00 PM. Additional meetings may be added when time before the competition is limited.

Garibaldi Secondary School - Room 2038

How?

You can join by:

  • Talking to Mr. Skartvedt
  • Signing up in the library hallway if available
  • Meeting us on Monday after school (Room 2038)
