1
Start of FTC at GSS Robotics September 2024

Our FTC team meets for the first time. Our team consisted of 6 members. We begin by participating and winning $1000 through the FTC Sim challenge.

2
L. A. Matheson Qualifier January 17, 2025

We compete in the L.A. Matheson Qualifier during the 24/25 season Into the Deep. We played 5 rounds and placed 19th out of 20. It was our first ever competition within the First Tech Challenge.

3
Start of Decode Season September 2025

At the start of the new season, Decode, we take apart our old robot and start again. We only have 5 members this season, and we used a collection of miscellaneous parts to build our robot.

4
Port Moody Qualifier January 31, 2026

We arrive at Port Moody Secondary to compete for a spot at the regionals. Building and programming our robot in between games, we played 5 matches and won 3 of them. We placed 9th of 25 and were one place short of the playoffs.

5
Initial Launch of This Website June 20, 2026

Our programmed designed and coded this website, going live at gssrobotics.github.io after 5 internal iterations. Within a couple days, the website rose to the first Google Search result for the term "gss robotics ftc".

6
Start of Biobuzz Season September 2026

We start our 3rd season full of hope. We begin planning to finally earn a place at regionals, and contacting potential sponsors for funding.

7
Next Steps: New Robot Parts Estimated time: October 2026

We are planning to buy a new Starter Kit, as well as strafer wheels and a REV Expansion Hub to put our robot on par with competitors in terms of physical and mechanical features. We are currently fundraising by reaching out to local businesses and other potential sponsors.

8
Next Steps: Sensing Capabilities Estimated time: December 2026

We plan to use a webcam and colour sensors to help our robot understand its surroundings and better perform during the autonomous period.
